The extension does not collect or extract TradingView market data, access brokerage accounts or credentials, monitor account activity, create or route orders, execute trades, or act on the user’s behalf. Strategy application occurs only when the user expressly requests it.

Finsec indicator output is informational. A forming or confirmed match is not financial advice, a guarantee of performance, or an instruction to trade. The user remains solely responsible for every trading or investment decision.

Uploaded data is stored temporarily for the active browser session and removed from extension storage after successful application. Data saved by a third-party platform remains subject to that platform’s storage, privacy, and usage terms.
